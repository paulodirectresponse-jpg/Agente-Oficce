# Office visual assets

## Pixel Interior
The interior tile and furniture sheets in `public/office-assets/` were imported from the public repository `ashawareb/the-office`.

That source project credits the furniture and interior tiles to **bitglow — Pixel Interior**, released under **CC0**.

Only the CC0 furniture and interior tile sheets are reused here. LimeZu character sprites from that repository are intentionally not included.

Source: https://github.com/ashawareb/the-office

## MetroCity characters
The role character strips in `public/office-assets/characters/` come from **MetroCity - Free Top Down Character Pack** by **JIK-A-4**, licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

Source pack: https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack
Imported from the CC0 copy documented in: https://github.com/holasoymalva/my-little-office

## Development V2 sprite kit
The sprite files in `public/office-assets/v2/` were imported from the open-source **Pixel Agents** repository maintained at `pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents`.

They are used only as the zero-cost asset-first validation kit for the Development vertical slice. The source repository is licensed under the **MIT License**; a copy of that license is included at `public/office-assets/v2/PIXEL_AGENTS_LICENSE.txt`.

Source: https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents
